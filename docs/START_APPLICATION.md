## About you / your background

I'm a software engineer who builds safety-critical, real-time systems for a living: software where a lost link
or a slow frame is never acceptable, and every component has to keep working on its last known state and recover
by itself. Outside that day job I design and ship B2B SaaS products with AI integrations: event-driven backends,
cloud infrastructure as code, and LLM features that return validated, structured output instead of free text.

I'm now taking that discipline to spatial computing. Most of my stack carries over directly: Kotlin/TypeScript,
real-time sync, resilient architectures, and production AI on AWS. What I'm adding is web-native XR, built on
Meta's Immersive Web SDK, so the experience opens from a single link with nothing to install.

## What are you building?

**SpatialDesk Command Center**: a hands-first productivity space for Meta Quest, delivered as a WebXR app built
with Meta's Immersive Web SDK. It is live at https://d2p3027seoreck.cloudfront.net (nothing to install).

You sit down, put on the headset and say or type your day. An AI agent (Amazon Nova on Bedrock) turns it into a
spatial workflow around you:
- Kanban boards floating at arm's length;
- focus timers you can pin anywhere in your space;
- cards you pinch, move and complete with your hands.

The agent doesn't just chat. It acts on the space, explains every step, and every step can be undone. It suggests
the next focus block when one ends and plans a new day from yesterday's open work. Every control also works by voice
(Amazon Transcribe) and by gaze alone, so the whole workspace can be used without hands.

The target is the Productivity track, Agentic Interaction and Accessibility. Every interaction is designed for
seated use, with large, high-contrast targets, and every quality claim is an automated test in Meta's IWER emulator.

## Why do you want to join Meta Horizon Start?

I want to build this the right way for Horizon OS from day one: performance budgets that hold at 60+ fps on
Quest, interaction patterns that survive real-world hand-tracking noise, and a first-five-minutes experience that
works for someone who has never used VR for work.

Start's technical education, office hours and community are exactly what a developer moving from cloud and
real-time systems into XR needs. They help me validate design decisions with people who ship on Quest every day.
In return I'll share what I learn: I document my builds in public, including a detailed friction log of the
tooling.
